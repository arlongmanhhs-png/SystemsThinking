# Step 8. Weigh consequences and interests

**Not yet written.** This file holds what is settled about the step: what it is
for, its critical check and its sources. What the participant does, in what
order and with what guidance, is written phase by phase in the working document
and comes here after it is settled. Do not build from this file alone.

## What the step is for

Makes the choice between candidates explicit as a value judgement.

## Output

Consequence and interests grid per candidate, choice with justification

## Uses the output of

Step(s) 1, 2, 6, 7. See `core/critical-checks.md` for the dependency table and
the return triggers.

## The critical check

The step is complete when:

- For each candidate: who gains, who loses, and what the loops suggest could go wrong.
- The choice is made and justified against stated values.

The criteria are self-checked. See `core/critical-checks.md`.

## Sources

Ulrich and Reynolds (2010); Stroh (2015), making an explicit choice. Full entries and verification status in `sources/sources.md`.
