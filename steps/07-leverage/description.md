# Step 7. Identify leverage

**Not yet written.** This file holds what is settled about the step: what it is
for, its critical check and its sources. What the participant does, in what
order and with what guidance, is written phase by phase in the working document
and comes here after it is settled. Do not build from this file alone.

## What the step is for

Finds the places where a change would alter the system's behaviour. This is where the parked list from Step 1 is opened.

## Output

Candidate leverage points, ranked by type

## Uses the output of

Step(s) 1, 2, 3, 5, 6. See `core/critical-checks.md` for the dependency table
and the return triggers.

## The critical check

The step is complete when:

- Each candidate points to an element of the mapping, and the actors who control it are named.
- Each is classified by Meadows' type, with its likely ripple effects.
- At least one candidate goes deeper than parameters (numbers, budgets, targets).
- Each trap found in Step 6 is rated twice: how strongly it holds the pattern in place, and how hard its way out would be to take, given who controls it.

The criteria are self-checked. See `core/critical-checks.md`.

Meadows (1999); GO-Science (2022), tool 8; Empower SDGs Step 5. Full entries and verification status in `sources/sources.md`.
