# Step 10. Test the hypothesis

**Not yet written.** This file holds what is settled about the step: what it is
for, its critical check and its sources. What the participant does, in what
order and with what guidance, is written phase by phase in the working document
and comes here after it is settled. Do not build from this file alone.

## What the step is for

Checks whether the hypothesis holds up once accumulations, delays and causal steps are made explicit.

## Output

Stock and flow sketch where relevant, theory of change with assumptions

## Uses the output of

Step(s) 1, 2, 3, 5, 9. See `core/critical-checks.md` for the dependency table
and the return triggers.

## The critical check

The step is complete when:

- Every link in the theory of change carries a stated assumption, and a theory of no change says what happens without the intervention.
- Where stocks or delays matter, a stock and flow sketch shows them.
- Weak assumptions send the participant back to Step 5 or 7.

The criteria are self-checked. See `core/critical-checks.md`.

GO-Science (2022), tools 9 and 10; Sterman (2000), testing; Empower SDGs Step 6. Full entries and verification status in `sources/sources.md`.
