8

Who is not in this list, and why not?

The gate: the step is complete when

  • What is excluded is stated, with a reason.
  • The actors include those affected but not involved.
  • Every actor sits in one layer and carries one type.
  • For every actor, what they hold is recorded. For the actors the dependency question applies to, who needs each of those holdings from them is recorded as well.
  • Which layer can take the decision, which is tasked with carrying the decision out, and which answers for the result, are each named or recorded as nobody.
  • At least two different descriptions of the problem are recorded.
  • The situation sketch has been drawn at the start, revisited and restructured at the end, and its three sentences written.

What the allocation in zone 5 usually shows

  • All three in one layer. Concentrated, and rarer than it looks. Step 12 then has one target and the ask can be simple.
  • Authority in one layer, responsibility in another. The common case in multi-level governance. An intervention aimed only at the responsible layer changes how the thing is done, never the rule under which it is done.
  • Accountability somewhere else, or nowhere. Expect blame to move and correction to be slow. That is a balancing loop, and it belongs on the mapping in step 5.
Before you finish, go back to the sketch with the second colour, rearrange it, and write its three sentences.
