# The process

Thirteen steps in five phases. Seven of the thirteen come from the Empower SDGs
programme; the rest close gaps in it.

Critical check criteria for each step live in that step's own folder, not here.
The machinery of critical checks, dependencies, and return triggers is in
`core/critical-checks.md`.

| # | Phase | Step | Output | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A. Frame | Define the problem, the desired change, and your position | Problem definition, desired change, the participant's position | Core |
| 2 | A. Frame | Set the boundary and the perspectives | Boundary statement, layer scheme, actor types, actor list with power, situation sketch | Core |
| 3 | A. Frame | Describe behaviour over time | Behaviour over time graph, system problem definition | Core |
| 4 | B. Understand | Identify enablers and inhibitors | Grouped factors, named as variables and typed as stocks or flows | Core |
| 5 | B. Understand | Map the causal structure | Causal loop diagram with polarities, loops, delays, stocks, and control markers | Core |
| 6 | B. Understand | Analyse the mapping and write its narrative | Named loops, archetypes, mental models, narrative | Core |
| 7 | C. Choose | Identify leverage | Candidate leverage points, ranked by type | Core |
| 8 | C. Choose | Weigh consequences and interests | Consequence and interests grid per candidate, choice with justification | Core |
| 9 | C. Choose | State the intervention hypothesis | IF / THEN / BECAUSE statement | Core |
| 10 | D. Test and plan | Test the hypothesis | Stock and flow sketch where relevant, theory of change with assumptions | Core |
| 11 | D. Test and plan | Plan monitoring and learning | Indicators, signals of change, review points | Core |
| 12 | E. Apply | Apply the analysis | Acting for an organisation: target actor, level, arena, instrument, ask. Advising: options with a recommendation. Deciding: a decision. Outside: findings | Core, framed by the position |
| 13 | E. Apply | Communicate to audiences | Message per audience | Core, framed by the position |

## The phases

- **A. Frame** (1 to 3): what the analysis is about, where its edges are, and what
  pattern it has to explain.
- **B. Understand** (4 to 6): what holds the pattern in place, drawn and then
  explained.
- **C. Choose** (7 to 9): where to intervene, at what cost to whom, stated as a
  testable hypothesis.
- **D. Test and plan** (10 and 11): whether the hypothesis survives being made
  explicit, and how anyone would know it is working.
- **E. Apply** (12 and 13): what the participant's position requires from all of
  it, and how it is told to the people who have to act.

## Folder names

Step numbers are permanent and are never reused. The folder names are:
`01-framing`, `02-boundary`, `03-behaviour-over-time`, `04-factors`,
`05-mapping`, `06-analysis`, `07-leverage`, `08-consequences`, `09-hypothesis`,
`10-test`, `11-monitoring`, `12-apply`, `13-communicate`.
